1. 2 cups fresh spinach leaves
2. 1/2 cup pomegranate seeds
3. 1/4 cup roasted almonds
4. 1/4 cup crumbled paneer (Indian cottage cheese)
5. 1 tablespoon lemon juice
6. 1 teaspoon honey
7. A pinch of black salt
WINTER 
SPINACH SALAD
•
Wash and chop the spinach leaves.
•
In a bowl, mix spinach, pomegranate seeds, roasted almonds, 
and paneer.
•
In a separate small bowl, whisk together lemon juice, honey, 
and black salt.
•
Drizzle the dressing over the salad and toss gently.                     
Serve immediately.

1. 2 cups shredded cabbage
2. 1/2 cup boiled green peas
3. 1/4 cup grated coconut
4. 1 tablespoon lemon juice
5. 1/2 teaspoon mustard seeds
6. 1/2 teaspoon urad dal (black gram lentils)
7. 1/2 teaspoon oil
8. A pinch of asafoetida (hing)
9. Salt to taste
CABBAGE AND 
PEAS SALAD
•
Heat oil in a small pan and add mustard seeds. Let them splut-
ter.
•
Add urad dal and asafoetida. Sauté until urad dal turns golden.
•
In a large bowl, combine shredded cabbage, boiled green peas, 
and grated coconut.
•
Pour the tempered mustard and urad dal over the salad.
•
Add lemon juice and salt. Toss well and serve.
